Zhuoxuan Li

Li Zhuoxuan / 李卓轩

I am pursuing a research-based master's degree at the School of Information, Renmin University of China, jointly supervised by Prof. Shaolei Zhang and Prof. Ju Fan.

My research interests include recommender systems, spatio-temporal data mining, and self-evolving agents. During my undergraduate studies, I worked under the supervision of Prof. Liang Hu. I also maintain close academic communication and collaboration with Jieyuan Pei from Zhejiang University of Technology and Yanxin from Beijing Normal University.

Away from research, I often find my rhythm on badminton and tennis courts, on a run, or in the gym. I am drawn to cinema as an art form, and hope to spend more time with books on theology. I also have a particular soft spot for white female singer-songwriters of Gen Z: in their lyrics, I often glimpse something of myself, finding words for feelings I have carried but could not quite name.
